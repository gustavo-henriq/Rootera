# Onboarding overrides

- **Glass on content is allowed here, and only here**: the experience cards are frosted glass over slow
  colour fields (`Atmosphere`). The fields exist so the glass has something to refract; with Reduce
  Transparency both disappear and the cards are solid.
- Progress is a plant growing (five shoots of increasing height), not dots.
- Steps: opening (automatic), how Rootera learns (tap each source), experience (glass cards),
  name (optional), nudges (kinds, tone, exact time; mornings recommended), first plant (specimen plate).
- The last step saves the plant and its first soil check, plays the seed drop with that species,
  then lands on Today with real guidance. No paywall at the end of onboarding.
